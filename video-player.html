How to install

  1. Tap Download APK and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  3. If Play Protect says the app is unrecognised, choose Install anyway. This appears because the app is not from a store yet.
  4. Open Zarvimo and allow access to your videos and music.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob.

Does it upload my videos?

No. Your videos and music are played from your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Read the Privacy Policy.

Which permissions does it need?

Access to videos and audio (read-only) to show your library, internet for streams and ads, and notifications (optional) for music controls.

I forgot my Child lock PIN.

Clear the app’s data in your phone’s app settings. This also resets favorites, history and settings.

Is the 200% volume boost safe?

High boost can distort sound and may hurt your ears or speakers. Increase it slowly, especially with headphones.

A video will not play.

The file may use a codec your phone cannot decode. Contact us with the file type and your phone model.
